3 STRUCTURING MISTAKES THAT COST YOU DEALS

Good morning,

When a file falls apart at the private level, it's almost never the borrower. It's usually a small structuring decision that could have gone differently.

Mistake #1: Submitting incomplete on day one.

We'd rather have a one-paragraph summary with the basics than a perfectly polished package three days later. Speed beats polish.

Mistake #2: Pitching the wrong exit.

"Refinance with a bank in 12 months" only works if the client's income story improves. If it won't, that's a different conversation — and we can structure for it.

Mistake #3: Hiding the messy stuff.

Bankruptcy, judgments, recent restructuring — tell us upfront. We've underwritten around all of it. What kills a deal isn't the issue; it's finding out about it at the lawyer's office.

The brokers we do the most volume with all do the same thing: they package files honestly, tell us the real story, and let us structure around it.
