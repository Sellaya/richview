NEW-TO-CANADA & CREDIT-CHALLENGED — WE'LL LOOK AT IT

Good morning,

Two file types we want more of: new-to-Canada and credit-challenged.

New-to-Canada:

  • Strong income but no Canadian credit history yet
  • Foreign income that doesn't fit the bank's templates
  • Recently arrived, looking to buy or invest
  • Has down payment, has the story, just doesn't have the on-paper Canadian profile

Credit-challenged:

  • Past bankruptcy, consumer proposal, or delinquency that has been resolved
  • Bruised credit from a specific event (divorce, business downturn, medical) that doesn't reflect the current borrower
  • Files where the equity and the current situation tell a different story than the bureau score does
We underwrite the whole borrower, not the bureau. If the equity is real and the story is honest, we can almost always structure something.
