5 SIGNS A FILE BELONGS WITH A PRIVATE LENDER

Good morning,

If you're not sure whether to send a file to a bank, a B-lender, or a private lender like us — here's a quick filter we share with the brokers we work with most.

Send it to us if:

  1. Timeline is the real constraint. The deal works financially but won't close fast enough through traditional channels.
  2. The story doesn't fit a bank template. Self-employed, BFS, recently restructured, complex income — all fine for us, problematic for the bank.
  3. Equity is strong but cash flow is tight (or vice versa). We underwrite the full picture, not a single ratio.
  4. The file is unique. Construction draws, land servicing, mixed-use, unusual property type — these are normal conversations for us.
  5. The client has been declined elsewhere — but the deal is still good. Most of our best files are deals that didn't fit somewhere else.
If you're on the fence about whether a file is "private deal" material, send a one-paragraph summary. We'll tell you in hours whether we can structure it.
