EQUITY TAKE-OUT vs. REFINANCE — WHICH TO PITCH

Good morning,

A question we get from brokers regularly: when do you pitch a client an equity take-out versus a full refinance?

Pitch a full refinance when:

  • The current 1st mortgage is at a rate worse than what's available today
  • The client needs to restructure the whole debt picture
  • Penalty math works in their favour
  • They want a single payment going forward

Pitch an equity take-out (2nd mortgage) when:

  • The existing 1st is at a low rate the client doesn't want to touch
  • Penalty would be punishing
  • The need is short-to-medium term (12-24 months)
  • The exit is a defined event (sale, business liquidity event, refinance later)
Quick rule of thumb: If preserving the existing 1st mortgage saves the client more than the cost of a 2nd, structure as a take-out. If not, refinance the whole thing. When in doubt, send us the file and we'll model both.
