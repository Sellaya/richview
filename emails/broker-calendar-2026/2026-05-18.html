BRIDGE FINANCING — THE WINDOW MOST BROKERS MISS

Good morning,

We see it every week: a client is buying before they sell, and the broker scrambles to make traditional financing fit a timeline that was never going to work.

That's a bridge file. And it's one of the strongest deal types we fund.

Bridge financing is the right call when:

  • Your client is purchasing a new property before their existing one closes
  • A renovation or repositioning needs to happen before refinancing
  • Timing matters more than the lowest rate
  • The client has equity but needs liquidity now, not in 90 days
Bridge files are time-sensitive by definition. When you submit early — even informally — we can usually give you direction within hours, not days.

What we need to move fast: a clear story on the exit, current property value, target purchase, and any existing financing.

If a bridge file is sitting on your desk right now, send it over.
