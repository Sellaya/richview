EQUITY TAKE-OUTS FOR BUSINESS LIQUIDITY

Good morning,

Equity take-outs are one of the most flexible tools in a broker's pocket — and one of the most under-pitched.

Reasons your clients might need one (that you can solve with us):

  • Renovations or property repositioning
  • Business capital injection for a self-employed client
  • Consolidating high-interest debt against equity
  • Buying a second property without disturbing the existing first
  • Covering a short-term cash flow gap during a transition
Our approach: we look at the equity position and the exit. If the file makes sense as a whole, we can usually structure it — even when traditional lenders pass on the income side.

If you have a client who needs liquidity but the bank is asking for income docs they can't produce, that's the conversation to bring us.
